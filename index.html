Democratic Republic of the Congo

The Democratic Republic of the Congo is a country in Central Africa. Its capital city is Kinshasa. The country is rich in natural resources and is home to the Congo River and the Congo Rainforest, one of the largest tropical rainforests in the world. The DRC is also known for its cultural diversity, beautiful landscapes, and wildlife.
